backchat is a Linux client for the bitchat protocol: serverless chat over a
Bluetooth LE mesh. Messages hop peer to peer with a short time to live, so the
room works with no internet at all; geohash channels and private messages ride
Nostr relays when there is one.

Wire compatible with the upstream bitchat iOS and Android clients: join the
same room as nearby phones without an account, a server, or a phone number.
